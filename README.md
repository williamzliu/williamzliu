## Hi, I'm William 👋

I'm a student at Stanford University studying Computer Science (AI) and Mathematics, and primarily interested in startups and venture capital.

I was a [KP Engineering Fellow][kp] with Kleiner Perkins and a [BCV Labs Fellow][bcv] with Bain Capital Ventures. Previously, I have interned at [Wisdom AI][wisdom], where I worked on natural language processing / machine learning to improve our AI data analytics platform. The summer before that I was at [Amazon][amazon], doing full stack software engineering for internal tools.
 
On campus, I lead a few student organizations: I'm president of the [Stanford Computer Science Student Council][cssc], a founding Vice President of the [Stanford Semiconductor Student Club][semi], and a Director of [Stanford Club Golf][golf]. Feel free to reach out via **[email][email]**!

Outside of school I enjoy playing golf (a highlight was meeting [Jim Nantz][article] at Pebble Beach through my involvement in [First Tee][firsttee]) and photography ([portfolio][photos])
